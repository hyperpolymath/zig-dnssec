= Zig 0.17.0 standard library: what DNSSEC needs and what is there
:toc:

Surveyed against the installed toolchain, Zig 0.17.0 (released 2026-10-01; tarball
checked against the published SHA-256 and minisign signature). Everything below was
read from `lib/std` of that release and exercised by the tests.

== Signature algorithms

[cols="1,2,3,2",options="header"]
|===
| No. | DNSSEC algorithm | std 0.17.0 | Used here

| 5, 7 | RSASHA1, RSASHA1-NSEC3-SHA1
| `std.crypto.Certificate.rsa.PKCS1v1_5Signature.concatVerify(modulus_len, …, Sha1)`
| yes

| 8 | RSASHA256 | same, `sha2.Sha256` | yes
| 10 | RSASHA512 | same, `sha2.Sha512` | yes
| 13 | ECDSAP256SHA256 | `std.crypto.sign.ecdsa.EcdsaP256Sha256` (Verifier with `update`) | yes
| 14 | ECDSAP384SHA384 | `std.crypto.sign.ecdsa.EcdsaP384Sha384` | yes
| 15 | ED25519 | `std.crypto.sign.Ed25519` (`verify` cofactored, `verifyStrict` cofactorless) | yes, `verifyStrict`
| 16 | ED448 | *absent* (no Edwards448/Curve448 in `std.crypto.ecc`) | no — documented gap
| 12, 23 | ECC-GOST, ECC-GOST12 | absent | no
| 17 | SM2SM3 | absent | no
|===

Notes on the RSA verifier:

* It lives in `std.crypto.Certificate`, i.e. it is the TLS certificate path, and is
  public API (`pub const rsa`).
* `modulus_len` is a comptime parameter. This library instantiates it for every
  modulus length in whole octets from the algorithm's minimum (64 octets, or 128 for
  RSASHA512) to 512 octets. That covers every size RFC 3110 allows, at the cost of
  roughly 80 s extra compile time in ReleaseSafe.
* `PublicKey.fromBytes` rejects moduli under 512 bits and exponents that are even, below
  3, or longer than 4 octets.
* Verification re-encodes EMSA-PKCS1-v1_5 and compares; it does not parse the decrypted
  block.

== Hashes

`std.crypto.hash.Sha1`, `sha2.Sha256`, `sha2.Sha384`, `sha2.Sha512`: all present. Used for
DS digests (SHA-1/256/384), NSEC3 hashing (SHA-1) and the signature algorithms above.
GOST R 34.11 and SM3 are absent.

== Networking and TLS

* `std.crypto.tls.Client`: TLS 1.2 and 1.3 client with certificate-chain and host-name
  verification against a `std.crypto.Certificate.Bundle`. Used directly by the DoH
  transport.
* `std.http.Client`: HTTP/1.1 only. *Defect found:* with an `http://` proxy configured,
  `connectProxied` returns the plain proxy connection after `CONNECT` and never starts
  TLS, so an `https://` request goes through the tunnel in cleartext. Reproduced with a
  minimal CONNECT proxy that logged `GET /dns-query HTTP/1.1` as the first tunnelled
  bytes. The DoH transport therefore does not use `std.http.Client`; it uses
  `std.http.Reader` and `std.http.Client.Response.Head.parse` for the response only.
* No QUIC and no HTTP/2: DNS-over-QUIC (RFC 9250) and HTTP/2 DoH are not implementable
  from std alone.
* No HPKE: Oblivious DoH (RFC 9230) is not implementable from std alone.

== Language and library changes met on the way (0.15 → 0.17)

* The `**` array-repetition operator is gone; use `@splat` or a comptime helper.
* `@typeInfo(T).@"enum".fields` is replaced by `field_names` / `field_values`; use
  `std.enums.tagName` and `std.meta.stringToEnum`.
* `std.mem.indexOf*` are now `find*` (the old names remain as aliases).
* I/O goes through `std.Io` (readers, writers, net, clocks, randomness); `main` takes
  `std.process.Init`.
* `zig fmt` rewrites `@intFromEnum` to `@backingInt` where it applies.
* Build: `b.args` is replaced by `Run.addPassthruArgs()`.
* `std.testing.fuzz` takes a `std.testing.Smith`; `zig build test --fuzz=N` runs a
  bounded coverage-guided campaign and saves crashing inputs under `.zig-cache/f/`.
